[IMDRF-MD-N66]
IMDRF — Medical Devices Regulatory Harmonization, N66 R1, Section 4.4
Essential Principles of Safety and Performance (EPSP) checklist must be
completed, demonstrating how the device meets each essential principle. The
checklist must reference the specific evidence (test report, design dossier,
or risk management file) for each principle.

---
[IMDRF-MD-N66.1]
IMDRF — Medical Devices Regulatory Harmonization, N66 R1, Section 4.5
Summary Technical Documentation (STED) must follow the IMDRF STED format
including: (a) device description; (b) intended use; (c) previous generation
comparison; (d) intended purpose statements; (e) alternative practices
comparison; (f) reference standards; (g) risk analysis summary; (h) review
of verification and validation activities.

---
[IMDRF-MD-N66.2]
IMDRF — Medical Devices Regulatory Harmonization, N66 R1, Section 4.6
Clinical evidence must follow IMDRF Clinical Evidence framework (GRRP N12):
(a) clinical evaluation plan; (b) clinical data appraisal; (c) clinical
evaluation report; (d) literature search protocol with reproducible search
strategy; (e) equivalence analysis if relying on predicate device.

---
[IMDRF-MD-N66.3]
IMDRF — Medical Devices Regulatory Harmonization, N66 R1, Section 4.7
Post-market surveillance documentation must follow IMDRF GRRP N14 framework:
(a) PMS plan defining proactive and reactive activities; (b) PMS report
analysing collected data with conclusions on benefit-risk profile; (c)
corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) summary.
